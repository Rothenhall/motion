Product design proposal · October 2026

From a set of tools to a calm studio for your content.

Motion already has the Cailyx look. What it lacks is structure built around the work: getting from an idea to a published post and learning from the result. This proposal puts the content first, joins the steps into one flow, and makes every number explain itself.

Scope All nine screens + shared componentsKeeps Graphite palette, serif headings, MontserratDelivery 4 phases, each one shippable
Where it stands

Polished surfaces, generic structure

A walk through every screen of the current app turned up five structural problems. None of them are about colour or type.

Every page is the same page

Hooks, Ideas, Automations and Inbox all use a form on the left and a list on the right. Nothing is shaped by what the page is for.

Content is hidden

Posts, reels and hooks are what Motion produces, yet they appear as one-line text rows. There are almost no thumbnails.

The core loop is broken up

Idea, hook, draft, check, schedule and review sit on six separate pages. Each step starts from an empty form.

Numbers without meaning

Stat tiles show a figure with no comparison, and the main chart is a single line. Only Pre-flight explains itself.

Empty states stop you

"No ideas yet" in a dashed box. There is no sample, no one-click start, nothing to learn from.

Research

What the leading tools get right

Six products set the bar for this category, plus the wider 2026 SaaS patterns. For each, the pattern worth taking and how it would land in Motion.

Later

Visual planning

The visual Instagram planner shows your profile grid as followers will see it, with scheduled posts placed among published ones. You drag media from a side library straight onto the grid or calendar.

For Motion: a Feed preview beside the Planner, and media you can drag onto a day.

Buffer

Ideas pipeline

The Create space stores ideas without choosing a channel first. Ideas are tagged, shown as a board or gallery, dragged between stages from idea to publish, and turned into a post when ready.

For Motion: merge Ideas and Hooks into one Content Lab board with stages.

Metricool

Best time to post

A heatmap with days as columns and time slots as rows, where a darker cell means a better time, built from your own past performance and shown inside planning.

For Motion: the same heatmap in Analytics, and its best slots shaded into the Planner.

Sprout Social

Smart Inbox

One stream for comments, DMs and mentions across profiles. Conversation history appears inline as you reply, handled messages drop out, and AI helps draft replies.

For Motion: a three-pane Inbox with context about the post and the person.

OpusClip

Explaining a score

The 0–99 virality score is split into Hook, Flow, Value and Trend, each with a letter grade, next to a written summary and a timestamped transcript. Their own guidance treats the score as a ranking, not an approval.

For Motion: Pre-flight leads with letter grades per factor, tied to moments on the timeline.

Linear

Speed and polish

Every action is one keystroke away through the command palette, widely seen as the benchmark for this pattern. Calm, dense layouts and quick transitions make the app feel fast.

For Motion: ⌘K grows from navigation into actions: "Schedule", "Check reel", "New idea about…".

2026 SaaS dashboards

Wider trends

Dashboards are moving from static reports to AI that surfaces what matters, bento grids that group content into scannable blocks, and modular widgets people arrange themselves.

For Motion: an Overview bento with one AI brief at the top and tiles you can rearrange later.

The common thread

Takeaway

The best tools in this category show the content itself, bring planning, checking and results into one place, and explain numbers in words. Feature lists across tools are now very similar, so the experience is where tools are told apart.

For Motion: Pre-flight and the brain view are a real differentiator. Build the studio around them.
Principles

Four rules for every screen

Content first

Thumbnails, reel frames and phone previews are the main visual. The interface around them stays quiet, in ink and grey.

One flow

A post is one object moving through stages: idea, draft, checked, scheduled, published. Every screen shows where it is and offers the next step.

Numbers that talk

Every figure comes with a comparison and one plain sentence, such as "Reels posted before 7pm reach 2.1× more people".

Calm motion

Soft staggered reveals, smooth reordering and slight hover lifts, all on Cailyx's single easing curve. Nothing bounces.

Shared components

Six pieces, built once

Every screen above is built from the same small set. Making these first keeps the redesign consistent and quick to roll out.

Post card

Thumbnail, channel, status, time and score. The most-used piece in the app, in compact, calendar and gallery sizes.

Three habitsIG reel · Today 6:30
Phone preview

A per-platform frame that shows caption cut-offs, aspect ratios and covers. Used in the Composer, Planner and Overview.

Insight line

One AI sentence plus an action. It is how numbers explain themselves across Overview, Analytics and Pre-flight.

AICarousels lead this month at 6.1%.
Detail drawer

A slide-over panel to inspect a post, idea or conversation without losing your place on the page.

Bento tiles

Standard, ink hero and media tiles on a 12-column grid, with Cailyx's staggered reveal on load.

Command bar actions

⌘K runs actions as well as navigating: schedule, check a reel, generate ideas, jump to a post.

⌘K › Check "Launch teaser"
Roadmap

Four phases, each one shippable

Ordered so that each phase is usable on its own and the shared pieces come before the screens that use them.

1Foundations

Shared components and data

Nothing new visible yet, but every later phase speeds up.

  • Post card, phone preview, insight line, drawer, bento tiles
  • Thumbnails stored and served for every post
  • Post stages (idea → published) in the data model
  • ⌘K actions
2First impression

Overview and Planner

The two screens people open most, and the biggest visible change.

  • AI brief, ink hero tile, week strip, what worked
  • Calendar thumbnails and drag to reschedule
  • Best-time shading and the feed preview
3Create

Content Lab and Composer

Joins the idea-to-post loop into one flow.

  • Merge Ideas and Hooks into one board with stages
  • Full-screen Composer with phone previews
  • Hook suggestions while typing, inline Pre-flight
  • Pre-flight grades, timeline and fix checklist
4Engage and learn

Analytics, Inbox and Automations

Closes the loop from results back to planning.

  • Story-style Analytics with heatmap and post gallery
  • Three-pane Inbox with drafted replies
  • Visual When → If → Then automation builder
Decisions needed

Questions before building

Merge Ideas and Hooks into one Content Lab?It simplifies navigation from nine items to eight, but changes where people find hooks today.
Full-screen Composer or keep the dialog?Full screen fits the previews and inline checks. The dialog is faster for quick text posts. Both could exist, with the dialog for text-only posts.
How much should the AI do on its own?This proposal keeps AI to suggestions and drafts that you approve. Auto-scheduling or auto-replying would be a separate decision.
Where to start?Phase 2 gives the biggest visible change soonest. Phase 3 delivers the most value for daily use.
